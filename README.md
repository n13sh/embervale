# Embervale

A 2D pixel sandbox adventure for **Android** and **Windows**, with LAN cross-play for up to 8 players.

Dig, build and explore a hand-crafted procedural world: forests, deserts, tundra, jungles, swamps, savannas, oceans, caves and the Underworld. Relight the fading Hearthflame by hunting down the beasts that guard the three Embers.

### Install

**Android**
1. Download `Embervale.apk` on your phone or tablet.
2. Open it from your Downloads and allow "Install unknown apps" if asked.
3. Tap Install, then open Embervale.

**Windows**
1. Download `Embervale.exe`. No installer needed.
2. Double-click it. If SmartScreen appears, choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

## What's in the game

- **The hunt.** One clear path through the story: nine bosses and minibosses in order, each one unlocking the next. The Journal's Hunter's Bestiary has a page for every beast: where it lives, how to find it, how it fights and what it drops.
- **Bosses in stages.** Main bosses stop mid-fight to talk, then come back faster, each with its own soundtrack and an Overdrive remix for the final stage.
- **Six biome minibosses**, including a hidden one: an ancient banyan that looks like any other tree until you take an axe to it.
- **Signature weapons.** Every boss always drops a trophy that is crafted into its own weapon.
- **Pirate raids** by sea and sky, villages that fight back, farming, animals, cooking, weather and seasons.
- **LAN co-op** between phones and PCs on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot.
- **Runs on modest phones.** Low / Medium / High graphics presets and a fast 720p mode.

## Controls

**Windows:** A / D to move, Space to jump, left mouse to mine, build and attack, 1-0 or the scroll wheel for the hotbar, E for the bag, J for the Journal, H to heal, Esc to pause. Gamepads are supported.

**Android:** floating joystick, jump, attack (auto-aims at enemies), heal and sneak buttons. The layout can be edited in Settings.
